One guild / Many paths

Where could you fit?

These are directions you can explore, not permanent assignments. You can try more than one, change your mind and bring your own strengths to the team.

01 / FLIGHT

Pilot & crew

Fly, navigate, escort or serve aboard another member’s ship. Learn how a crew works as one.

02 / GROUND

Field operations

Move with a squad, communicate clearly and look out for the people beside you.

03 / INDUSTRY

Mining & logistics

Survey, mine, haul and support the people and ships that keep an operation moving.

04 / SUPPORT

Medical & rescue

Respond when a teammate needs help and make sure the crew can get home.

05 / DISCOVERY

Exploration

Scout new places, plan routes and make room for the unexpected parts of the journey.

06 / LEADERSHIP

Team coordination

Help people work together, welcome new members and guide a group when you are ready.

Our approach

Progress is shared.

No one has to arrive as an expert. A veteran can teach a beginner; a beginner can bring a new idea. We become a stronger guild when members help each other improve.
