Who we're looking for

Play as a crew.
Grow as a crew.

We're looking for players who communicate, work with their crew and put the team ahead of personal glory. You don't need to be an expert, but you should be willing to listen, coordinate and help others.

We plan together and look out for one another. Reckless solo runs and ego-driven play aren't the JMBN way.

New players are welcome. You don't need a particular ship, rank or career path to introduce yourself.
